# Hand-worked demo payroll references

These are test oracles for the Engine owner, not production tax code. Fixtures use
**FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27, new regime**, deliberately fixed to a documented historical
year. Do not silently reuse them for a different year. Sources checked 2026-10-25.
No claim is made that the official interactive calculator was exercised; the Phase 1
B3 task still requires cross-checking at least five incomes there.

Kestrel's variable amount is intentionally the balancing amount for exact 14 LPA.
Nimbus's INR 150,000 joining bonus and up to INR 50,000 relocation reimbursement
are outside recurring CTC. Employee PF is deducted once from gross; it is not a
second employer CTC component. These payroll cases exclude joining payments and
RSU realization; model those events separately. PF amounts are explicit fictional
contract terms, not a universal legal assertion. Gratuity provision is not cash in hand.

## Worksheet method

Assume a resident individual with only ordinary salary income, full-year employment,
no additional deductions or exemptions. Salary = fixed gross + realized variable.
Subtract INR 75,000 standard deduction, then round taxable income to the nearest
INR 10 (section 288A). Slabs used: nil to 400,000, 5% of the next 400,000, 10% of the
next 400,000, 15% of the next 400,000. No example reaches the higher bands.
For taxable income up to 1,200,000 apply the eligible rebate; just above that limit
cap pre-cess tax at the excess over 1,200,000 when marginal relief applies.
Add 4% cess, then round final tax to the nearest INR 10 (section 288B).

Annual net = annual gross - annual tax - 21,600 employee PF - 2,500 professional tax.
Average monthly net = annual net / 12, rounded to paise. PT is 200 in eleven months
and 300 in February for these two non-exempt demo employees.

Example A target: taxable 1,515,000. Tax = 400,000*5% + 400,000*10% +
315,000*15% = 107,250. Cess = 4,290. Net = 1,590,000 - 111,540 - 21,600 -
2,500 = 1,454,360. Example B half: taxable rounds to 1,229,780. Slab tax is
64,467 but marginal relief reduces it to 29,780; with cess 30,971.20, rounded tax
30,970. This rebate boundary is an essential future golden test.

## Three monthly slips per offer

Use target annual variable payout, paid in March; year-long projected TDS is spread
over twelve months. This is an explicit test payroll allocation, not a claim about
every employer's TDS practice. Kestrel rounds eleven TDS installments to 6,169.17
and reconciles the final one to 6,169.13, totaling exactly 74,030.

| Offer/month | Cash gross | Employee PF | PT | TDS | Net cash |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A April | 110,000 | 1,800 | 200 | 9,295 | **98,705** |
| A February | 110,000 | 1,800 | 300 | 9,295 | **98,605** |
| A March | 380,000 | 1,800 | 200 | 9,295 | **368,705** |
| B April | 105,000 | 1,800 | 200 | 6,169.17 | **96,830.83** |
| B February | 105,000 | 1,800 | 300 | 6,169.17 | **96,730.83** |
| B March | 194,554 | 1,800 | 200 | 6,169.13 | **186,384.87** |

Ten ordinary-month slips + February + March reproduce the annual target net.
References are stored as literal expected values in `mocks/expected_payroll.json`,
not generated by the eventual finance implementation. Includes early-exit cases at
months 11 and 12, RSU cliff and zero-price cases. `mocks/expected_claims.json` holds
the fictional policy worked examples. B4/B5 should turn these into engine golden tests.
